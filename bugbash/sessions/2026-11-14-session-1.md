## Checked, working as intended

- Weekly bank across a mid-week goal change: 1,814 + 500 − 500 + 100 = 1,914 kcal banked; the bank resets
  on Monday.
- Protein: 99 g of a 100 g target is a miss; the streak counts complete days only and breaks on unlogged
  days.
- lb ↔ kg round trip is exact (175.4 lb → 79.56 kg → 175.4 lb). Moving a weigh-in onto a taken date is
  refused.
- With the profile time zone set to UTC+14, MCP calls without a date log to the user's local "today".
- Backup download is a valid SQLite file (`integrity_check` ok). CSV and JSON exports are complete.
- 120 concurrent writes from two "devices": no errors, and totals are exact.
- Hostile input (SQL and HTML injection, 10k-character strings, NaN, 1e308, emoji) on every endpoint and
  MCP tool: no 5xx, every error is JSON.
- The official MCP Inspector CLI lists and calls tools over Streamable HTTP. The stdio bridge works.

## Deferred

- USDA `DEMO_KEY` runs out quickly (about 30 requests/hour; the live step hit it). The app explains this and
  points to the free key in Settings. Results are cached for 7 days. Recommend setting `USDA_API_KEY`.
- The Open Food Facts search index (search-a-licious) has low-quality names for some US store brands.
  USDA branded data is better for Costco products; results show USDA first.
- Camera barcode scanning needs HTTPS; typing digits works. A reverse proxy with a local certificate would
  enable it later.
